Our Story

From one classroom
to three branches.

Apex Academy was founded in 1994 in Guwahati with a singular focus: prepare students for India's most competitive exams. We started with a small batch of students preparing for SSC, and quickly built a reputation for honest, intensive, and results-driven teaching.

By 2005, we had expanded into APSC — the Assam Civil Services — and over the years added programs for Banking, Railway, Assam Police, and Foundation (Classes 9 to 12). Today we operate from three branches across Assam and have trained more than 50,000 students.

Throughout this growth, we have never compromised on what matters most: the quality of teaching in the classroom. Every Apex student receives personal mentorship, regular assessments, and a learning plan tailored to their strengths and weaknesses.

Milestones

A timeline of growth.

The moments that shaped Apex Academy — from a single classroom in 1994 to the institution we are today.

1994

Founded in Guwahati

Started with a single classroom and a small batch of SSC aspirants. The first year produced a 100% selection rate in the SSC batch.

2000

First 100 Selections

The institute crossed 100 cumulative selections in SSC CGL, marking a regional milestone.

2005

APSC Vertical Launched

Expanded into Assam Civil Services, building a dedicated state-specific curriculum and interview-preparation cell.

2010

Second Branch Opened

Opened the Jorhat branch to serve students from upper Assam, marking the start of our multi-branch journey.

2015

Banking & Railway Programs

Launched dedicated verticals for Banking (PO / Clerk) and Railway (NTPC / Group D) to meet rising regional demand.

2020

Digital Classrooms

Introduced hybrid classrooms, recorded lectures, and a digital test series — keeping students learning even through disruption.

2025

Assam Police Vertical

Launched a dedicated Assam Police program covering AB, UB, SI, and APS roles — both written and physical preparation.
