Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the study materials provided?
Yes, comprehensive study materials designed by our expert faculty are included in the course fee. These include concept books, practice workbooks, and current affairs magazines.
Can I pay the fees in installments?
Yes, we offer flexible installment plans for our year-long foundation batches. Short-term crash courses usually require one-time payment.
Do you offer hostel facilities?
While we don't have our own hostels, we have tie-ups with verified, safe PGs and hostels within walking distance of our campus for outstation students.
What if I miss a class?
Students can access recorded backup classes through the student portal, or attend a doubt-clearing session over the weekend.
